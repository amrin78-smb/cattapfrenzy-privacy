Privacy Policy

Introduction

Cat Tap Frenzy ("the App") is developed and operated by Amrin bin Abdul Rahman ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

By using Cat Tap Frenzy, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Cat Tap Frenzy is a casual mobile game. We do not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. However, the following data may be collected by third-party services integrated into the app:

  • Device identifiers and advertising IDs (collected by Google AdMob for ad serving)
  • Approximate location data (used by AdMob to serve region-appropriate ads)
  • App usage and interaction data (used by AdMob to improve ad relevance)

Game scores and leaderboard entries are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any server.

Advertising

Cat Tap Frenzy uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. AdMob may use cookies and device identifiers to serve personalized advertisements based on your interests.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this app and other apps and websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Cat Tap Frenzy is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app's ad serving is configured for a general audience and does not target children specifically.

Data Storage

All game data including your scores, leaderboard entries, and achievement progress is stored locally on your device using your device's built-in storage. We have no access to this data and it is not shared with any third party.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. AdMob's privacy policy can be found at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data including the right to access, correct, or delete data held about you by third parties. Please refer to Google's privacy controls to manage data collected by AdMob.
